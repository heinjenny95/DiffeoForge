# Pilot review and iteration headroom

The pilot distinguishes a quick method screen, a serious local fit and visual
suitability. A numerical iteration limit is an intermediate result; it does not
mean that a shape or a fitting hypothesis has passed.

## Complete geometry for decisions

The inspection window loads the complete original and reconstruction in the
background. A display proxy remains a loading view. Suitability, rejection,
relative feedback and problem marking wait for the matching complete models and
a settled frame. Navigation temporarily disables decisions. A load failure
provides a retry or a way to close without recording.

Saved alternatives remain optional. Only the selected alternative loads. Relative
feedback waits for every displayed comparison to be complete; hiding alternatives
allows the current fit to be assessed on its own. No display mesh replaces the
scientific surface.

Closing stops the display timer and cancels pending selected-comparison loading.
Each modal caller disposes the window after copying its feedback. Repeated
reviews therefore do not accumulate hidden parent-owned windows and full models.

## Local trials receive more headroom than the screen

New protected regional trials allow at least 400 iterations. Several marked
areas, a measured miss requiring broad capture, or a new residual field raise
that initial allowance to at least 600. A larger inherited allowance is retained.
The policy uses the actual regional request and measured capture distance, not
triangle count, species names or an anatomical feature detector.

These are initial allowances, not sufficient iteration counts for every shape.
The native solver may stop earlier at its tolerance or a reported failure.
Changing the allowance does not change the objective, numerical tolerance,
integrator, control basis or numerical guards. Existing prepared runs retain
their declared caps.

An inspected, finite regional result that reached its cap can receive a separate
same-objective continuation when its bound progress and preservation evidence
qualify. Its accepted field and marks are retained, with new optimizer history
and a larger allowance. There is no cumulative one-test-per-setting ceiling.
Failure, anatomical suitability and numerical convergence remain distinct; a
failed optimizer is never silently recovered.

After a substantial inspected local pass, a rejection for remaining detail can
recommend finer independent local movement while retaining the improved broad
field. This route requires the existing trusted continuation gate, preserved
shape and transition checks, and measurements for every marked problem area.
The worst remaining area determines capture; an improved large area cannot hide
a smaller miss in the union statistic. Surface capture stays broad while a
marked surface remains out of reach, and protection is not weakened. Explicit
longer optimization remains available. This is a new fitting hypothesis that
still needs numerical and visual validation, not an anatomical acceptance rule.

## An unresolved method comparison can move to another form

Two inspected, technically valid Current and Varifold results can be compared on
the next untested selected pilot form even when their details still need work.
This explicit provisional action grants neither suitability nor coverage credit.
It prepares fresh fields on the next form and preserves earlier feedback and
marks in their original history. Starting-method and immediate-refinement choices
remain available. When the selected forms are exhausted, the pilot explains how
to choose an inspected starting method or refine an existing result.

The existing flow for two genuinely approved fits remains separate. Preparation
does not start a fit or grant approval for a common atlas or PCA.

## Input guidance reuses existing inspections

The background presentation checks only the selected original/template pair
against their current content hashes and existing inspection cache. Differences
in surface connectivity or openings trigger a plain-language input-review hint.
Matching cached counts are not a topology certificate, and an unknown cache
result starts no validation or mesh parsing. The hint does not change a recipe,
block geometric improvements or infer that an external projection is impossible.
The existing original/template inspection remains directly accessible.

## A failed numerical trial retains a safe route forward

After a qualified terminal finite-accepted-guard failure, the pilot can inspect a
verified earlier completed fit and explicitly prepare a different test from it.
It never imports the failed field or checkpoint, changes the failed status, or
manufactures an approval. Original marks and failure history remain bound.

Known terminal failed objectives are compared within their immutable starting-fit
and mark scope. Returning to an identical earlier failed objective is rejected in
the preview, fresh preparation and saved-successor verification. Choosing a
different fit or changing the marks does not relabel old failures. Unknown or
changed terminal evidence permits diagnosis rather than an inferred recovery.
Preparation and Run remain separate, with fresh idle/terminal UI checks.

## Verification boundary

Saved-field temporal verification can now reach 577 timepoints when the earlier
73, 145 and 289 levels do not meet the unchanged maximum-displacement threshold
of 1e-4 relative to the original template diagonal. Stable earlier levels stop
immediately. The retained-frame check and the final check after original-source
spatial refinement use the same limit and threshold. An unresolved final level
remains unqualified; additional timepoints do not manufacture suitability.

These are Float64 RK2 evaluations of the same saved field, with zero optimizer
updates. They change neither fitted coefficients nor a prepared fit's declared
integration grid or iteration allowance. Larger full-surface trajectories require
more memory; a resource failure remains explicit and preserves prior evidence.

Regression checks exercise actual Qt decision gating, selected-alternative
switching, ordinary guided preparation and generated native iteration limits.
Historical numerical-successor and rendering checks retain their earlier grids
and objectives. These checks do not establish anatomical success across arbitrary
datasets; varied real-form assessment remains a separate empirical requirement.

Saved-attempt labels also preserve the scope of conservative comparisons: a
regional outcome can flag a whole-shape regression even while the marked area
improves. The UI does not describe that combined flag as proof that the marked
area itself became worse. Per-area and whole-shape evidence remain available;
no regression threshold is relaxed.
