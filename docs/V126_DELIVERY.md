## Boundaries

Software, packaging and provenance checks do not qualify an optimal control
density or native scientific performance. Each new fit still requires its own
researcher review, and the selected state requires its integration and completion
gates. Full-cohort atlas execution/QC and PCA remain separate future actions.
The independently deployed institutional-server continuation and its scientific
sources/settings are untouched. Drive distribution intentionally remains at its
previous verified build until an explicit upload request.
