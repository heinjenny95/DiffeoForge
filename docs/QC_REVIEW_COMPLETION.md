# v82: completing visual QC with concerns

Required visual review and a favorable scientific judgment are separate decisions.
After inspecting both source-resolution meshes, a researcher may record Plausible,
Uncertain or Implausible. Every required case still needs a mesh-bound inspection
acknowledgment and a decision. Missing inspections and changed evidence remain blockers.

`Finish review & view with QC warnings` records explicit access to exploratory results
when concerns remain. Negative decisions are preserved; no specimen is excluded, no
atlas/PCA output is recomputed, and no failed registration becomes a pass. Results
show a persistent caution; scientific reports retain every subject and its decision.
The full-cohort plausibility claim is not supported with any implausible case and
is at most partial with uncertainty or incomplete cohort review. Share QC status
alongside standalone plots, which remain unchanged source artifacts.

The v0.3 result-access record uses `flagged-registration-visual-review-v2`, binding
the finalized review, screening evidence, mesh inspections and concern subjects.
Valid v0.2 all-plausible releases remain readable under their original strict policy.
Drafts are not migrated or rewritten just by opening them.

Disabled ratings explain the pending original-detail render or inspection checkbox.
This does not relax the source-resolution inspection gate. Relative residual screening
can miss uniformly poor fits: unflagged and uninspected specimens remain unreviewed.

For an implausible fit, inspect the existing alignment, starting template and local
reconstruction before deciding whether to make a separately documented corrected run.
Numerical convergence is not anatomical plausibility. No automatic repair, exclusion,
parameter change or atlas rerun is triggered by finishing QC.
