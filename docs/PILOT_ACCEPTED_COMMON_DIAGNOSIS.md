# Common-model experiments against accepted individual shapes

The guided common path compares a completed, inspected ordinary field with the
explicitly accepted individual shape of the same specimen. Input, template,
cohort, inventory, review and reconstruction identities remain bound. Regional
references use their verified complete-field endpoint; a residual alone or a
display proxy is not substituted. Both original-to-fit and fit-to-original
distances, two deterministic area-sampling repeats, spatial tails, existing
accepted marks and direct common-to-accepted agreement inform the comparison.
Sparse repeat-confirmed features remain visible even when global P95 hides them.

A previous matching common field provides a geometric trend. Improvement toward
the accepted shape can justify another bounded 120-step continuation. Local or
whole-shape regression vetoes that recommendation. At the first comparison,
recorded objective progress provides limited supporting evidence; an accepted
protected local correction instead motivates a capacity contrast. These are
testable hypotheses, not unique causal diagnoses or anatomical decisions.

The complete accepted field's recorded control budget is a floor for the shared
capacity test. Preserved prefixes and added local residual fields are counted
together; a later refinement of the same field is not counted twice. All accepted
single shapes contribute, and the highest-demand form is tested first. Local
counts are never discarded merely because their fitted momenta cannot be copied
directly into the shared model. This is a capacity policy, not proof that a count
is a mathematically necessary or sufficient model size.

When a shared field already exists, the capacity contrast retains every old
control and momentum and appends zero-momentum controls. Its count reaches at
least the recorded accepted demand; a later capacity contrast can increase by
50%. A missing shared field starts directly on the larger candidate basis.
The 2,000-control guard is explicit; an over-budget demand is not silently reduced.
Kernel, attachment, precision, tolerance,
integration, solver and other numerical settings remain fixed. The allowance is
120 steps, with fresh optimizer history and explicit cumulative effort. At most
two same-basis continuation rounds precede the capacity alternative; one bound
capacity experiment per accepted target/protocol is reused rather than duplicated.
Human non-improvement feedback remains a veto. Old starting-fit selectors cannot
bypass the common recommendation through optional unchanged optimization.

Original individual comparison references survive later common approvals. The
chosen common protocol and earlier decisions remain saved during an experiment.
A successful capacity candidate needs visual approval and explicit model
adoption. That revision does not transfer approvals to changed results. Other
specimens can retain their own inspected fields on the same explicitly adopted
nested basis, with zero added momenta and new review, rather than restarting cold.
Separate fields are never spliced and another animal's momenta are never reused.

Before the first common phase, the largest accepted transferable direct nested
basis with unchanged fitting physics is preferred as the seed. If that basis
falls below complete-field demand, a larger common candidate is prepared first,
including when the demand came from local corrections. An already active shared
protocol is not silently reselected; the larger candidate needs explicit adoption.

Unavailable comparison evidence is identified explicitly; corruption fails its
identity checks. Sampling agreement does not grant suitability. Preparation runs
no optimizer: the existing explicit Run action and responsive cancellation remain.
Prepared/running historical contracts, installed versions and delivery receipts
retain their separate provenance. No timed review pause, cap increase or numerical
accuracy relaxation is introduced.

Qualification includes public triangular surfaces with a narrow missed feature,
reverse-direction protrusion signals, marked-area preservation, sampling
uncertainty, mixed regression vetoes, source/approval/cache tampering, bounded
experiment selection and complete preparation/review/adoption/transfer journeys.
Read-only private saved-field checks and exact-package delivery are recorded
separately. Newly proposed private native experiments still need execution and
independent anatomical review; diagnostic success is not a fit-quality guarantee.
