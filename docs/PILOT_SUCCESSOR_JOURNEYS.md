# Continue a reviewed individual successor

The pilot previously stored a Better rating as a working-fit preference but did
not consistently use it when choosing the next action. A historical local
trade-off could therefore keep reopening a comparison even when a verified
correction of the newly marked area was available. A second mismatch allowed
feedback-only inspection to present a next step whose dispatch expected a formal
unsuitable review.

dev220 consumes the verified current-field preference in both paths. When a new
red area can seed an existing protected correction, that correction takes
priority over passive historical comparison. Green-area source confirmation,
selected-source alignment/topology concerns and numerical qualification remain
separate checks. Suitable reviews continue to advance coverage; Better alone
does not approve a fit. Fresh preparation rechecks the exact field, values,
marks and any proposed iteration allowance. Preparation still starts no fitting.
For an eligible individual repair, the fixed footer can proceed after verified
feedback-only inspection; the secondary action says Reopen inspected rather than
claiming a formal decision. Common and joint results retain their review gates.

An eligible, still-improving capped fit can offer its existing continuation.
New marks without matching saved regional measurements do not become evidence
of local improvement: the pilot can offer a separately qualified local test
instead. This change does not raise any limits or change tolerance, geometry,
initialization or fitting algorithms. Ordinary progress presentation still uses
native log precision; a rounded plateau is not new evidence of convergence.

Saved-fit comparison preparation now distinguishes empty-message exceptions
from success, names missing comparison metadata, coalesces duplicate requests and
cleans up after cancellation or closing. Study changes, replacement operations,
workers and review-saving prevent stale results or choices from being applied.
A reader already running may finish after cancellation, but its stale result is
discarded. Cancellation does not falsely claim that native parsing stopped.

Verification covers complete controller journeys from method selection through
a finer successor, Better feedback, local marking or capped continuation and
actual successor preparation. Formal unsuitable and feedback-only inspection
paths are separate cases. Worse, Unsure and Suitable outcomes retain their
distinct behavior. Qt lifecycle checks cover compare, cancel, close, failure,
replacement and stale callbacks. A mutation-denying replay of the reported
saved state verifies the exact selected source and local action without changing
inputs, reviews or native evidence. These engineering checks do not establish
anatomical improvement, independent first-use acceptance or scientific validity.

Combined scoped verification covers 228 distinct cases. Two older expectations
looked for a duplicated body preparation button; they were updated to verify the
fixed footer or explicitly open the optional adjustment disclosure. The affected
paths passed follow-up checks. This test/documentation follow-up changes no
runtime code; the dev220 binary remains bound to its tested feature commit.

Two additional common-feedback tests expect an older broad/detail recovery
label instead of the current candidate-refinement route. The same failures were
reproduced against the verified dev219 package, with its strategy-state reader
unchanged. Reconciling those existing policy expectations remains separate from
this individual-pilot repair; no common-model approval rule was relaxed.
