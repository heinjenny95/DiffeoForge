# Local feature correction: material and qualification

A marked target projection can intersect only a tiny sliver of the saved fitted
surface. Nonempty source selection alone does not establish that the local
objective contains a useful amount of movable material. Inspect the selected
source and target patch areas, connectivity, and the actual supported controls
before spending another iteration allowance.

The missing-source fallback grows a connected free patch to the marked target
area when the original source brush is empty. New protected-capture preparation
also covers an insufficient nonempty brush: grow from the existing free patch
along shared edges, excluding every triangle incident to an explicit Retain
vertex. The contrast is deterministic, records its source faces and areas, and
does not infer anatomical correspondence. Default selection, shape-preservation
guides, sufficient brushes and explicit Retain overlap keep their existing scope.
The new preparation records its material-selection version and any partial-brush
extension. Historical plans, fitted fields, reviews and shared-model coverage stay
unchanged. Installed application delivery is tracked separately from this source
change.

An unsupported extra projection needs the opposite surface-distance direction:
measure fitted surface to original as well as original to fit. The correction
region must include the unwanted material and a nonempty original surface patch.
Previously good projections need independent preservation checks. Whole-surface
percentiles alone can hide either kind of local failure.

New local-improvement measurements compare the worse of the original-to-fit and
fit-to-original mean distances. The worst current sampling repeat must improve
by more than five percent against the best baseline repeat. This recognizes an
extra fitted projection being retracted, while preventing an improved forward
distance from hiding a worse reverse distance. Both directional measurements
remain visible: improvement of this symmetric summary does not mean that every
local distance improved. Preservation, tail-distance, intersection, temporal and
spatial gates remain separate, as does researcher approval. Previously saved
measurement records are not rewritten.

New isolated correction plans retain the complete qualified starting field as a
fixed prefix, fit a zero-initialized residual through the existing native regional
route, and evaluate the complete composition from the original material grid.
The bounded new-step allowance, numerical accuracy, cancellation and storage
reserve remain explicit. No timed review pause or success-driven cap increase is
introduced. Synthetic checks cover a nonempty sliver, insufficient disconnected
material, fixed-incident exclusion, deterministic selection, default/guide
compatibility and unchanged older plans. The source selection exactly replays
the separate native qualification contrast. Preparation checks alone do not
qualify anatomy: native execution, temporal/spatial checks and independent
anatomical review remain separate.

The run-plus-resume disk-reserve check remains mandatory. If it blocks dispatch,
retain the prepared case and resolve storage first. Owner-authorized archiving of
selected inactive build copies can free space after complete destination/source
hash verification and restoration checks. Do not bypass the reserve, silently
choose another network destination or resume a separately paused dataset batch.
Private cases and detailed native receipts remain outside this public repository.
