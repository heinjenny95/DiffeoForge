# Keep accepted individual fields when preparing the joint check

Previously the adaptive pilot transferred shared parameter values and the chosen
control grid to the joint check, but removed the fitted individual momenta. The
individual trials fixed template and controls; the joint trial restored the
original shared estimation flags. Thus individually suitable forms could lead to
a substantially worse joint result from a different starting point. Returning to
an accepted individual fit could prepare the same fresh joint trial again.

New adaptive joint checks bind all accepted individual fields on the exact same
original template/control basis. Every field must retain its original target,
cohort, method, effective parameters, model, precision and integration settings.
The existing frozen-singleton binder verifies the completed native inventory,
finite coefficients and original control locations. Regional residual chains and
incompatible bases cannot silently become ordinary joint initializers. Failure
does not substitute a fresh start.

Coefficients are copied without fitting or projection. Subject blocks follow the
platform's canonical input-path order, independently of pilot selection order;
the declared subject list and staged native engine are checked. Reopening or
running rechecks bound source/copy hashes and exact matrix assembly. Template and
control-point estimation flags, optimizer, precision, tolerance and iteration
allowance remain unchanged. Optimizer history starts anew. This initializes a
new joint optimization; it does not splice fields into an existing atlas or PCA.

A visible accepted-fit return selects a verified same-form owner-approved field
without running a fit or approving a joint result. A joint reconstruction cannot
be offered as the frozen singleton seed for protected local fitting. Joint
recovery uses its own source-bound event; ordinary same-form selection gates
remain strict. The joint view states whether its fields start fresh or from
accepted results. Repeating an already rejected joint setup with identical
accepted fields, settings and basis is blocked in preparation. Changing a bound
starting field is a different trial, not an identical repeat.

New joint results compare every form's sampled surface agreement with its
accepted individual baseline. Regressing forms are named in the UI. These checks
do not grant or remove anatomical approval; every joint form still needs review.

89 focused source checks, including synthetic journeys, verify explicit recovery,
no optimization/approval during
selection or preparation, strict basis/order/coefficient binding and unchanged
shared estimation flags. Copied-input native CPU evaluations additionally verify
the assembled accepted state, a fresh state and the earlier joint state, with
zero optimizer updates. The actual product staging reproduces the independently
assembled initial objective exactly. Source fields and existing fit evidence
remain unchanged. This establishes initialization correctness, not final fit
quality or a runtime speedup. A new owner-run joint check remains necessary.
