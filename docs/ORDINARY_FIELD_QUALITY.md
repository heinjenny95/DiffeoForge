# Original-source qualification of ordinary singleton fields

New guarded full-target singleton results receive a separate fixed-field
qualification before their evaluated endpoint is measured and presented. Native
coefficients, exports, inputs, prepared contracts and earlier decisions stay saved.
This check performs no optimization and creates no anatomical approval.

The procedure binds the original source and target, native reconstruction, run
manifest, original control basis and accepted momentum export. Protected geometry
hashes are checked before and after evaluation. A changed input or reconstruction
cannot acquire a replacement quality binding.

The same coefficients are evaluated with native Float64 RK2 on nested grids of
37, 73, 145, 289 and at most 577 time points. Successive endpoint differences must
meet the existing relative maximum threshold of 1e-4. The optimizer's actual grid
is independently compared on the final material mesh with the existing RMS/P95/
maximum bounds of 0.005/0.01/0.02. Native export reproduction retains the existing
roundtrip RMS/maximum limits of 1e-5/1e-4. A stable fine grid cannot hide an
inaccurate optimizer grid; a failed check does not start another optimizer.

Source refinement splits original triangles conformingly, preserves old material
points and evaluates the unchanged field only at new points. It never smooths,
repairs or subdivides the fitted endpoint. Whole-surface edge/contact checks are
bounded to 500,000 vertices and 16 refinement passes per validation grid. Earlier
regional evaluations retain their default marked-area behavior. The final checked
surface must have no detected intersecting or nonpositive faces.

The ordinary quality record has its own key and version. Comparisons and preview
views use its hash-bound endpoint; an ambiguous or changed endpoint fails closed.
Qualification verifies the generated endpoint and material-source digests around
each comparison and before publishing the quality record atomically. If either
asset changes during checking, the saved native fit remains available and the
qualification attempt produces no quality record.

The separate joint field qualification applies the same identity rule to its
generated comparison meshes, final per-subject endpoints and shared material
source. The completed joint native run remains available if a generated mesh
changes during qualification. The joint quality record is published atomically
after the final identity and cancellation checks.
Failed numerical checks remain visible and veto replacing a retained starting fit.
Cancellation propagates through fixed-field shooting and geometry workers while
the completed native fit remains available. Existing preview approval policy and
optional original-detail loading are unchanged.

Qualification includes 85 distinct focused checks across the new validation,
measurement/presentation, source-refinement, regional field/material rules and
feedback paths. Two identity counterexamples preceded their correction. A real
completed public product run also passes export reproduction, source refinement,
time stability, actual-grid accuracy and final surface checks with zero optimizer
updates. This does not qualify new cohort orchestration, shared refined output
grids, template learning, PCA or anatomy; those remain separate gates.
