Surface accuracy also depends on representation and regularization: deformation
and attachment kernel widths control different spatial scales
([Durrleman et al., 2014, section 2.6.2](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4871626/)).
Currents can underrepresent sharp projections through orientation cancellation
([Charon and Trouve, 2013](https://arxiv.org/abs/1304.6108)); this is a general
mechanism, not a diagnosed cause in every regional fit, including those already
using a varifold focus term. More iterations of a fixed objective are not an
anatomical accuracy guarantee. Finer hypotheses and any replacement compute
runtime still require direct geometry, equivalence and stability verification.
