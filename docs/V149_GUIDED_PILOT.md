# v149 — guided template, coverage and fit decisions

The pilot now starts with a small comparison on one independent target and grows
only through explicit user actions. This replaces the six-setting, whole-pilot
batch for newly prepared automatic desktop projects. Existing studies keep their
original protocol; **Prepare new guided pilot** creates a separate successor and
retains their evidence. Preparing, opening, reviewing and changing a plan never
launch a scientific fit. The full cohort still requires its own atlas and QC.

## User route

1. In the input step, optionally select **Suggest a template**. Background
   measurements rank coarse radial/spectral shape centrality among usable
   surfaces. Preview, inspect alternatives, then explicitly use a candidate.
   Existing manual templates are preserved until that action. Continue with the
   existing alignment and mesh-quality checks.
2. Analyze aligned inputs. **Recommend size from form diversity** replaces a fixed
   eight-target default. The geometry medoid and farthest-first additions cover
   new shape directions; similar extremes are not repeatedly added automatically.
   Manual required specimens and declared biological strata/extremes remain
   binding. Turn the checkbox off to choose a manual total. Inspect the plan's
   coverage and omitted-neighbor explanations before execution.
3. Prepare the pilot. The initial batch contains **Current and Varifold**, one
   matched fresh trial each on the same independent specimen. Their meanings and
   comparison purpose are visible. The template and initial control grid are
   shared. The selected template is not itself a target.
4. Run the declared batch, then inspect one fit overlaid with its original.
   Saved alternatives load only when requested; synchronized camera and extent
   support comparison. Optional precise region marking on the original writes
   specimen/hash/coordinates into the review notes. General named features remain
   available for any surface dataset; no anatomy-specific counting rule is used.
5. Accept a suitable method, or record that both are suitable/unsuitable. **Both
   good** prepares another contrasting one-specimen pair. **Both unfit** leads to
   shared input/template checks and a targeted follow-up. **Unsure** leaves the
   decision open and exposes inspection help. A tied visible quality result is
   not turned into a fabricated scientific winner; recorded runtime can inform
   an explicit efficiency choice once fits are acceptable.
6. Targeted alternatives change one parameter family: finer attachment detail,
   broader deformation reach, or stronger attachment weight. Changes reset
   cross-check eligibility. Prior results remain available. The chosen settings
   are checked on the remaining selected forms one at a time, then one original-
   target joint pilot is prepared. Each prepared batch needs its own Run action.
7. Review every joint reconstruction. Reuse that exact joint fit through the
   historical parameter-stage bookkeeping into the existing fixed-state time-
   resolution checks; do not run those historical grids. Preserve optimizer-cap
   warnings and the existing explicit provisional-finish gate. Finishing prepares
   the full-cohort configuration; it does not launch an atlas or PCA.

## Policy and reproducibility

- Starting-template selection uses intrinsic coarse descriptors before alignment;
  it makes no unapproved anatomical reorientation, reflection, scaling or mesh
  repair. Finite nondegenerate geometry is a basic usability check, not complete
  mesh-quality certification. Symmetric/local-detail ambiguities remain possible.
- Pilot coverage uses the existing aligned, area-weighted multiscale descriptor.
  The default novelty threshold is 20% of the observed descriptor diameter, with
  a suggested budget of six and at least two separate targets. Required cases can
  raise the budget. These are versioned engineering heuristics, not validated
  biological sample-size or accuracy guarantees. Uncovered diversity at the
  budget is disclosed. A larger cohort does not automatically imply more pilots.
- The default screening limit is twelve prepared trials, 120 iterations each.
  Explicitly larger manual/mandatory cohorts raise the declared limit to at least
  target count plus two. Extra pairs reserve room for remaining cross-checks. Method
  pairs use identical originals, template, controls and settings; native objective
  magnitudes across methods are never ranked. Common original-surface/regional
  geometry guides inspection. Iteration-limited screens are provisional.
- Separate manifests bind source inputs, controls, settings, scope, history and
  prior explicit reviews. Withdrawn approval or changed run/settings invalidates
  dependent evidence. Per-transition/run locks prevent duplicate dispatch; stale
  locks require explicit recovery. No visual approval is transferred to a new fit.
- Scalar measurement caches retain content/transform/settings/version identity.
  A bounded 256-entry metadata cache avoids repeatedly parsing unchanged VTK
  coordinate arrays after fresh streaming SHA-256 checks. No full mesh cohort is
  retained. Cold checks, changed transforms and fresh hash I/O remain necessary;
  end-to-end large-cohort timing/RSS qualification remains open.
- Recording completion retains its encoder until the producer thread has exited.
  A queued saved/failed signal can arrive earlier; releasing its GUI-affine QObject
  from the producer creates a lifetime race. Completion is deferred with a GUI timer,
  preserving normal 10x actions, 2x pilot review and 1000x loading/computation.

## Verification boundary

Regression and synthetic controller/UI checks cover preparation without execution,
matched one-target trials, tie/rejection routes, targeted changes, distinct-form
coverage, explicit legacy upgrade, reopening, budget/lock guards, withdrawn reviews,
joint-to-final retention, lazy comparison, manual overrides and content-cache
invalidation. Native fitting is blocked in these software checks. Existing geometry
and numerical safeguards remain in force. Prospective native-method comparison,
independent first use, anatomical correspondence, selector sensitivity and
full-cohort scientific acceptance remain separate open gates.
