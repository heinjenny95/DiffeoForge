# Pilot capacity, effort and the exact review target

High or extreme expected disparity now reaches the guided workflow itself. A new
adaptive method comparison starts with a regular control grid whose nominal
spacing matches the declared deformation kernel. Its actual point count is
bound to the template and stored with the candidate. A target used by the older
screening-grid heuristic was a bound, not a promise of that many points; thin,
elongated shapes could receive substantially fewer controls.

Changing deformation width while retaining an unsuitable coarse grid can make a
local-movement hypothesis misleading. New high/extreme fresh movement trials
therefore compare the movement width with a matching control spacing. Extreme
disparity uses wider movement contrasts; it does not decide that local movement
is biologically preferable. Compatible retained-field continuations, approved
coverage and joint checks keep their explicitly chosen control basis.

The resource guard remains 2,000 controls. An automatic proposed movement scale
that exceeds it may use the nearest wider movement candidate within the stated
direction. The selected width, matching spacing and actual count are disclosed.
The grid is not silently coarsened while claiming to test the original kernel.
An explicit configuration outside the limit is not rewritten. There is no
iteration-cap increase, precision/tolerance relaxation, solver replacement or
automatic anatomical approval. Prepared/running historical contracts retain
their grids, parameters, limits and numerical behavior.

Capacity is a hypothesis, not a guarantee of useful correspondence. A low mean
or whole-surface percentile can coexist with a missing narrow projection or an
unsupported thin protrusion. Inspect the important original features, previously
good areas and transitions. Private engineering comparisons include complete
original-surface and marked-area measurements; they do not authorize a common
atlas or PCA. Shared-model and independent anatomical qualification remain open
when a difficult feature is still wrong.

The ordinary common-model allowance remains 120 additional iterations per
prepared continuation. Optional optimization cannot bypass a newer unchanged or
worse common-model result by selecting an older starting fit. Further comparable
continuations require feedback. New effort markers count the verified saved-field
branch separately from the restarted native counter. Earlier optional runs that
recorded only a local counter can contribute their separately recorded seed's
effort to a prospective successor. Historical counters and already-prepared
offset contracts are not rewritten; no uninterrupted optimizer trajectory is
claimed. Unrelated discarded experiments are not added to that branch count.

The fixed review action names the specimen, test and scope: individual, common
model or joint. Its destination is explicit, independent of a selected earlier
starting fit. Pending common-result review has one primary action; earlier
starting-fit editing and comparisons remain secondary. A reviewed result can
still be reopened explicitly. Suitable-fit next-step preparation preserves the
existing approvals and does not start fitting. A stale view cannot dispatch a
review to another study. A separate local repair retains its individual scope
even while the shared model is stalled; shared-model context alone does not
relabel that repair as common-model evidence.
Existing ordinary form-model checks and continuations retain their shared scope.
While a saved common result awaits review, returning to an accepted individual
fit remains available as a secondary action rather than a competing primary one.

Verification covers prospective actual grids, bounded movement selection,
unchanged numerical settings, chosen-basis transfer through coverage/joint
preparation, common-model non-improvement through the older-fit route, versioned
and legacy effort ancestry, and Qt review dispatch without fitting. The saved
other-method inspection callback captures its own reference rather than a later
history/input-check reference. Isolated native comparisons and exact-package
delivery evidence are tracked separately from software-contract tests and owner
anatomical approval.
