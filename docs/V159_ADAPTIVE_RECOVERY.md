# Feedback-driven pilot recovery

Repeated improvement attempts could become smaller changes and then exhaust a
one-test-per-hypothesis list. Changing a suggestion also synchronously reverified
the saved study ancestry in the Qt event loop. These are separate defects: slow
selection did not establish that an optimizer was stuck.

## Behavior

- **About the same:** keep the human-preferred comparison and increase the
  parameter contrast or test another explanation. A first compatible change is
  still gentle; subsequent relative levels are 90%, 63%, 44.1%, etc. of the
  retained value. Increasing-direction tests use the inverse progression.
- **Worse:** retain the earlier reference and suggest another family. An explicit
  alternative remains available. A parameter is not exhausted by one test.
- **Better:** the inspected new field can become the starting reference without
  being anatomically approved. Compatible field initialization, exact target and
  basis bindings, and the whole-shape regression guard remain required.
- Repeated density tests extend the most recent inspected, non-worse compatible
  field with zero-momentum controls while retaining the earlier comparison.
  They preserve the initial velocity, not optimizer history or a future trajectory.
- A newly capped field can receive a separate numerical completion test without
  replacing the retained comparison. An unchanged source cannot repeatedly receive
  the identical extension. Other parameter tests remain selectable.
- Source-bound marked patches persist through feedback-only review and reopening.
  An explicit Clear remains empty. Their physical scale and symptom inform the
  suggested test; local and global errors are measured separately. Marks are
  **not local objective weights** and do not identify a unique parameter cause.

There is no cumulative trial cap or once-per-family gate. Guided contrast levels
and the 2,000-control resource guard prevent extreme automatic suggestions;
reaching one parameter's guard leaves alternatives and input/template checks.
Each action prepares one test on one form, with a separate explicit **Run**.
Changed settings still require visual checks on the remaining forms and a joint
confirmation. No anatomical approval is inferred from feedback or distances.

## Responsiveness and verification boundary

The worker verifies shared inputs once and prepares every selector preview.
Changing a selector only reads that presentation table. Every actual Prepare
action revalidates current evidence; previews never authorize a transition.
Tests exercise repeated feedback, rollback, marked-patch persistence, numerical
seed/comparison separation, repeated nested density, selector callbacks with
verification forbidden, and evidence tampering. Synthetic controllers cannot
establish anatomical benefit. No owner/native fit is launched by this change.

Independent visual assessment of subsequent owner-run fits remains necessary.
