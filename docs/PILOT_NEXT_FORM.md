# Continue after a suitable individual fit

A suitable regional fit could be saved and inspected, but a fit with a composed
residual field had no usable next-form route. Repeated review did not resolve
that dead end. Completed-result inspection was also buried in scrolling history.

dev174 keeps **Review finished fit** outside the scrolling content. After an
explicit suitable-fit decision, **Use suitable fit → next pilot form** saves that
individual decision and prepares the next unreviewed animal. Computation still
requires **Run prepared test**. The completed fit remains the review target even
when an earlier starting fit was previously selected. Earlier fits and further
refinement remain available behind optional disclosures.

Individual anatomical decisions and common-model checks have separate counts.
An immutable progress record binds the reviewed source, original common template
and control basis, precision, integration grid and convergence tolerance. The
next animal starts a new individual field on that original basis and common
surface protocol. No previous animal's momenta, residual chain, problem marks or
anatomical approval are transferred. The approved composed fit stays intact as
an individual result; it does not become an ordinary common-atlas field.

After all individual shapes are reviewed, any missing standard-model checks are
explicit separate preparations. Only reviewed ordinary fields with matching
method, parameters, template/control basis, precision, integration and tolerance
can count toward joint initialization. The joint result still needs its own
review. Local fields cannot be spliced into an atlas or PCA. Choosing a newly
reviewed standard protocol can change the common-model coverage count while
preserving earlier shape decisions. A failed source verification shows its input
issue and keeps inspection available instead of silently choosing a fresh fit.

The next-form/common-model screen retains the existing 120-iteration allowance.
New attempts have no timed review pause, following the owner's October 31 update;
historical prepared contracts remain supported. Neither iteration limits nor numerical
accuracy are weakened. This repair does not establish that one standard protocol
will reproduce every individually optimized local fit or guarantee a runtime.

Qualification uses public synthetic controller receipts through remaining forms,
separate common-model checks and joint preparation; source/basis and approval
binding guards; Qt review/dispatch/scroll and live-worker gates; light/dark and
larger-font screenshots; and a write/execution-denying replay of the saved owner
metadata. No owner trial is prepared, run or approved by qualification. Native
objective and optimizer equations are unchanged. Packaging and installed state
are recorded separately.
